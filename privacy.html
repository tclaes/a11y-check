Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who we are

a11y-check.eu is a free, open-source web accessibility checker. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you visit the site, how we use it, and what rights you have. For questions, please open an issue on GitHub.

2. Data we collect

URLs you submit for accessibility checks

When you enter a URL into the checker, that URL is sent to our serverless function to perform the audit. We do not store submitted URLs or audit results. They are processed in memory and discarded immediately after the response is returned to your browser.

Analytics data (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site — which sections are read, how often the checker is used, and where visitors come from. Google Analytics collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • General geographic region (country/city level)
  • Device type and browser
  • Referring website

This data is aggregated and anonymised. We do not use it to identify individual users. See Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to show relevant ads based on your browsing history and interests. Google acts as a data controller for advertising data. You can review and manage your ad personalisation settings at adssettings.google.com.

3. Cookies

This site uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics to measure site usage (e.g. _ga, _ga_*).
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense to serve and measure personalised ads.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking analytics and advertising cookies will not affect your ability to use the accessibility checker.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics — Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — Privacy Policy
  • Netlify (hosting) — Privacy Policy

5. Data retention

We do not retain any user-submitted data. Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default, after which it is automatically deleted.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data held about you. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests relating to analytics or advertising data should be directed to Google. For any other questions, contact us via GitHub.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
